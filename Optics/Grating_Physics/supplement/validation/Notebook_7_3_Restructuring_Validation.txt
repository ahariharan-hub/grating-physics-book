NEW HELPERS

wave_optics/gaussian_order_beams.py
  Normal incidence sets k_ix=0; m*2*pi/d sets the central transverse wavevector.
  Each beam uses helmholtz_families.gaussian_beam after a right-handed rotation:
    e_l=(sin(theta),0,-cos(theta)); e_q=(-cos(theta),0,-sin(theta)).
  Coordinates (r.e_q, y, r.e_l) rotate envelope and all phases consistently.
  Waist centres coincide at the origin, but each waist plane is perpendicular
  to its own axis. The boundary is NOT a finite Gaussian multiplied by a mask.
  The axisymmetric helper is sampled at y=0; this is not 2D slit scattering.
  Equal coefficients are assigned; no grating efficiencies are calculated.

  The carrier phase gradient is exactly k*e_l. The full phase gradient at the
  waist centre is (k-1/z_R)*e_l because the Gaussian includes negative Gouy phase.
  Its direction agrees with the transmitted wavevector; its magnitude has the
  expected paraxial correction. Positive curvature phase and negative Gouy
  phase are preserved under the exp(-i*omega*t) convention.

  The API requires w0/lambda >= 3 and axes within 30 degrees of global -z.
  Local paraxiality and detector obliquity are distinct restrictions.
  Nonpropagating, grazing and out-of-scope axes are rejected.
  Spatial overlap is the normalized magnitude integral on the detector's y=0
  line. It is not full XY overlap, optical power, mutual coherence or visibility.
  A zero arm gives NaN overlap and zero cross term; magnitude normalization
  protects nonzero weak-arm overlap from simple strength-induced underflow.

wave_optics/gaussian_order_beams_view.py

finer fringes. The boxed same-wavelength plane-wave reference is

that wavelength has no effect on beam angles or Gaussian propagation.
