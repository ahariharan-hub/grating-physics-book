MODEL AND REUSE

The incident boundary field is exp(-x^2/w0^2) exp(i*k_x_i*x), peak amplitude 1.
A centered, 50%-open ideal binary-amplitude mask prescribes its transmitted
boundary. binary_transmission.binary_transmission_field supplies the mask;
binary_transmission_coefficients identifies its nonzero ideal harmonics.

Propagation reuses scalar_propagation.transfer_function and spectrum_geometry.
The existing utility propagates into +z at positive distance. Here its distance
is downward depth s=-z: signed transmitted k_z is minus its outgoing root, giving
exp(i*k_z*z)=exp(i*sqrt(k^2-k_x^2)*s). Evanescent components decay.
The new 1D FFT orchestration does not introduce a second dispersion/ASM kernel.

The default is lambda=1 um, d=4 um, w0=8 um, normal incidence, pair (0,+1).
The explorer computes depths 0..80 um at 1 um steps; detector motion reuses the
computed complex maps. It has six parameter controls plus one reset button.
The illustration above the grating is a geometric envelope extension; only its
boundary and the transmitted field below the grating are numerically propagated.

APPROXIMATIONS AND NUMERICAL DIAGNOSTICS

The model is scalar, monochromatic and invariant along y. It prescribes a
thin-mask field, not an electromagnetic scattering/efficiency solution.
Integrated transverse intensity is per unit y length, not total optical power.

The default grid has 64 samples per period, half-cell sample locations avoiding
mask jumps, and a padded power-of-two domain at least 512 um wide and at least
12*w0+5*s_max. The displayed maps crop x to +/-48 um. FFT spectra are unshifted
raw NumPy weights and preserve complex phase. Inverse FFT and propagation use
the same sampling convention.

Approximate order windows are disjoint half-open intervals between neighboring
ideal-center midpoints, clipped to strictly propagating |k_x|<k. Even nonzero
orders of this particular 50%-open mask have zero ideal coefficients and are
not offered as strong isolated beams. Filtering is numerical, not modeled
Fourier-filter hardware. Strongly overlapping replicas cannot be attributed
uniquely to orders by this prescription.

Warnings cover an isolated Gaussian replica midpoint amplitude above 0.01,
windows touching the grazing cutoff, outer-domain intensity fraction above
1e-4, and significant near-grazing spectrum whose estimated travel can reach
the FFT boundaries. A small edge fraction alone is not a wraparound guarantee.
Padding and sampling comparisons remain necessary for quantitative work.

The overlap O is the normalized integral of |U_m||U_n|, not mutual coherence.
It integrates the entire padded x grid and is undefined for zero-norm fields.
It is not assumed monotonic for arbitrary parameters. Propagating-spectrum
intensity norms and normal scalar flux are conserved; the full boundary field
also contains evanescent norm which decays without transporting normal flux.
